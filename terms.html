Replies and automations

When you accept, decline or answer maybe, Cadence sends that answer from your device to the provider that sent the invitation, after a ten-second window in which you can undo it. Providers can delay or refuse a change; Cadence shows when a reply is not confirmed, but you remain responsible for checking that an answer you care about arrived. Shortcuts you choose to run when a meeting starts or ends run under your control.
