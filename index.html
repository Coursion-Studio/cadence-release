    01Invitations

    Answer it where you see it

    Accept, Maybe or Decline goes out through your own account, and the next calendar refresh confirms it. Every reply waits ten seconds, so Undo always takes it back.

    Click a reply, or press A M D. ↑ ↓ pick the row.

    02Join

    Never miss the start

    The menu bar counts down to your next meeting. Join is one click, a double-click on the countdown, or the panel hotkey tapped twice from anywhere. On a meeting in any view, a double-click joins too.

    03Pre-meeting card

    Walk in knowing the room

    Five minutes before a meeting with people or a link, the menu bar panel leads with Join, who is coming and the agenda from the notes. It can open itself two minutes before, without taking focus.

    • Who has accepted, at a glance
    • The agenda pulled from the invitation’s notes
    • A time-sensitive reminder a minute before, with Join on it

    Their time, on the rail

    Day and week views carry extra zones down the hour rail, half-hour zones and daylight-saving days included. The inspector shows a meeting’s start in each of them.

    Move across the day, or focus it and use ↑ ↓.

    08Agents and Shortcuts

    Ready for your agents

    An MCP server runs inside the Mac app, on your machine only, behind a token in your Keychain. A cadence command line speaks to it, and Shortcuts, Siri and Focus plug in too.

    • Ten tools: next meeting, agenda, inbox, conflicts, free slots, the brief and more
    • Reads are always on. Replies from agents stay off until you allow them
    • Run a Shortcut when a meeting starts or ends
    • A Focus filter switches the calendar set and can hide declined events
    • “Hey Siri, join my next meeting in Cadence”

    Beyond the meeting

    The rest of your day is on it too.

    Dentist at ten, a birthday on Friday, a trip in nine days. Cadence keeps them where you already look.

    Reminders

    Apple Reminders sit beside your events with their due times. Tick one off in the agenda, day or week.

    Weather and birthdays

    A forecast on each day, and birthdays from your contacts, so a rainy Saturday and a friend’s party show up before you plan around them.

    Countdowns

    Pin a trip, a launch or a birthday and see the days left in the agenda, the menu bar and a widget.

    Editing

    Drag an event to a new time, or open it and change the title, repeat, alerts, people and calendar.

    Why Cadence

    Native. Direct. Free.

    A calendar for the meetings other people put on it and the plans you make yourself, that keeps your accounts to itself.

    Native on both

    SwiftUI on macOS 26 and iOS 26, sandboxed, from the App Store. Settings follow you through your own iCloud.

    Your own accounts

    Replies go from your device to the provider that sent the invitation. No Coursion server, no proxy.

    Keychain only

    Sign-in tokens and app-specific passwords live in your Keychain. No analytics. Read the privacy policy.

    Free, all of it

    Replies, reminders, weather, join, conflicts, widgets and agents on Mac and iPhone. No account and no subscription.
